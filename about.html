local_fire_department THE SHALIMAR FLAME

Born from Lahore’s Irresistible Obsession with Pure Taste.

In 2011, Hadiq Foods began with a solitary live charcoal pit along Shalimar Town’s pulsating food strip. The mission was straightforward: revive the pristine northern Shinwari method—relying strictly on hand-slaughtered meat, real tomatoes, organic desi ghee, and searing charcoal heat without artificial powders or shortcuts.

As Lahore’s night owls and younger generation sought out modern comfort food after 1:00 AM, we made an audacious decision: instead of treating fast food as an afterthought, we mastered it. We applied the exact same butcher-direct beef standards, freshly baked milk brioche buns, and high-temp cast-iron sear plates to engineer Lahore’s most addictive smash burgers.
