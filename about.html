ABOUT HADIQ FOODS • LALPUL, MUGHALPURA

Two Culinary Worlds.
One Passionate Lahore Kitchen.

Hadiq Foods & Pizza is a family restaurant in Lalpul, Mughalpura, Lahore. From pizza, burgers, shawarma and paratha rolls to BBQ and desi karahi, we serve both fast food and desi food from one kitchen — open every day until 4 AM.

From the Grill

Bar-B-Q & Naan

Chicken boti, kastori boti with cheese, chicken tikka and our Special BBQ Platter, served with roghni naan.

Desi Favourites

Karahi & Pulao

Chicken and mutton white karahi, chicken tikka masala and Bannu beef pulao.

Fast Food

Pizza & Burgers

Pizzas in five sizes, zinger and beef burgers, shawarma, sandwiches and paratha rolls.
